# Example Procurement Policy

**Synthetic demonstration data — not an actual company policy.**

Effective date: January 1, 2026. Organization: Example Research Company.

- Purchases below $1,000 require approval from the requesting team's manager.
- Purchases from $1,000 through $10,000 require manager and finance approval.
- Purchases above $10,000 require finance director approval and three vendor quotes.
- Employees must disclose conflicts of interest before selecting a vendor.
- Purchase orders must be approved before a commitment is made.
- Procurement records must be retained for seven years.

Exceptions require written approval from the finance director.
